FlipGoal: Football Pinball Privacy Policy

FlipGoal is a Nuco Games title. This policy explains what happens to your data.

NO ACCOUNT, NO PERSONAL DATA

FlipGoal does not ask for your name, email, contacts or location. Your level, gold and items are stored only on this device.

ADVERTISING

FlipGoal may show ads provided by Google AdMob. Google may use your device's advertising ID and basic device information to show and measure ads. You can reset or limit ad personalisation in your device settings. Google's policy: policies.google.com/privacy

CHILDREN

FlipGoal is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

YOUR CHOICES

Uninstalling the game deletes all saved progress.

UPDATES

This policy may change with new versions of the game.
